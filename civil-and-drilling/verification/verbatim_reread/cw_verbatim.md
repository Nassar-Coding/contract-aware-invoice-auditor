## CW p3 (footer: "Page 3")

(Page heading: PART I — GENERAL CONDITIONS)

**2. Subcontract documents**
This Agreement, the General Conditions, Schedules 1 to 5 and Appendix A together constitute the whole agreement between the parties. In the event of conflict, a Schedule prevails over the General Conditions, and a later-numbered Schedule prevails over an earlier one.

**4. The Site**
The Site is divided into four zones for the purposes of measurement, described in Schedule 2. The zone applicable to an item of work is the zone in which the work is physically executed, irrespective of where materials are stored or plant is based.

**5. Ground conditions**
The ground is classified into five categories described in Schedule 3. Classification shall be determined by the Engineer from the trial pit records and confirmed in writing before the work is executed. A classification once confirmed shall not be revised retrospectively.

**7. Working hours**
Normal working hours are 06:00 to 18:00 on working days. Work executed between 18:00 and 06:00 is Night Work and attracts the uplift stated in Schedule 4 where, and only where, that Schedule marks the item as eligible.

**8. Rest days**
The rest days under this Subcontract are Friday and Saturday. Work executed on a rest day attracts the rest-day uplift stated in Schedule 4 where the item is marked as eligible. No item attracts both the night uplift and the rest-day uplift on the same measurement unless the Engineer has instructed the work in writing under Clause 9.

**9. Instructed out-of-hours working**
The Engineer may instruct work outside normal working hours. An instruction shall be in writing and shall state whether the night uplift, the rest-day uplift, or both are to apply.

**11. Setting out**
The Subcontractor shall set out the Works from the primary control stations. Setting out is measured under item E.52.010 by chainage band and only against a joint survey signed by both parties.

**13. Testing**
The Subcontractor shall carry out the tests described in the Specification. Where Schedule 5 makes a test certificate a condition of payment, no valuation shall include the item until the certificate has been delivered.

**14. Defective work**
The Engineer may instruct the removal and re-execution of defective work. Re-execution is at the Subcontractor's cost and shall not be measured again.

**17. Dayworks**
Work instructed to be executed on a daywork basis shall be recorded on daywork sheets signed daily by the Engineer's representative. Daywork sheets not signed on the day of the work shall not be admitted.

**18. Plant and standing time**
Standing time is not payable except (a) where the Works are suspended by reason of adverse weather, in which case item E.51.010 applies against the meteorological record, and (b) plant standing measured under item E.51.030 against the plant standing record, each as required by Schedule 5.

**19. Traffic management**
Traffic management is measured by the day under item E.51.020. Not more than one day of traffic management is measurable per work area per day, irrespective of the number of locations at which signals are deployed.

(Page heading: PART II — MEASUREMENT AND VALUATION)

**25. Principle of measurement**
The Works shall be measured net as executed in accordance with the Civil Engineering Standard Method of Measurement, as modified by these Clauses. Where a Clause of this Part conflicts with the Method of Measurement, this Part prevails.

**26. Units of measurement**
Each item in Schedule 1 states the unit in which it is measured. No item shall be measured in any other unit, and a quantity presented in a unit other than that stated shall be rejected in its entirety rather than converted.

**27. Build-up of rates**
The rates in Schedule 1 are base rates. The sum payable for a measured quantity is ascertained by applying to the base rate, in the following order and no other: first the site zone factor from Schedule 2; then the ground classification factor from Schedule 3; then the night uplift, if applicable; then the rest-day uplift, if applicable; then the cumulative quantity rebate from Schedule 4, if the threshold has been passed; and the resulting sum is then rounded.

**28. Rounding**
Rounding shall be applied once only, to the rate after the whole of the build-up described in Clause 27 has been completed, to the nearest halala, a half halala being rounded upward. Rates shall not be rounded at intermediate steps. The amount for a measured quantity is the quantity multiplied by the rounded rate; where Clause 30 divides a quantity at a rebate threshold, each part is multiplied by its own rounded rate and the amount is their sum.

**29. Application of factors**
A factor applies to an item only where Schedule 2 or Schedule 3 lists that item's series. An item whose series is not listed is measured at the base rate irrespective of the zone or ground classification in which it is executed.

**30. Cumulative quantity rebates**
Where Schedule 4 states a rebate threshold for an item, the rebate applies to that part of the quantity which exceeds the threshold when taken cumulatively across the whole of the Works to date, and not to the quantity below it. The cumulative count runs from the Commencement Date, is not reset by a valuation, and is taken in order of the date of execution; measurements executed on the same date are taken in order of application number and then line number.

**31. Daily limitations**
Where Schedule 4 states a daily limitation for an item, no more than that quantity is measurable for that item, per work area, per day. A quantity in excess of the limitation is not payable and shall not be carried forward to a subsequent day.

**32. Mutually exclusive items**
Where Schedule 4 states that an item is excluded by another, the excluded item is not measurable within the stated number of days following the measurement of the excluding item on the same work area.

**33. Surveyed items**
Items marked as surveyed in Schedule 1 are measured only against a joint survey sheet signed by both parties. The quantity measured shall be the quantity on the survey sheet exactly.

**34. Provisional quantities**
Quantities in Schedule 1 are estimates and do not limit the quantity that may be measured.

**35. Work not measurable**
Temporary works, dewatering below the classified water table, and the removal of the Subcontractor's own waste are deemed included in the rates and are not separately measurable.

**36. Disputed measurement**
Where the parties cannot agree a measurement, the Engineer's measurement shall be used for the purposes of interim valuation without prejudice.

**37. Re-measurement**
The Contractor may re-measure any item within ninety days of the valuation in which it was first included.

**38. Currency**
All rates are in SAR and all valuations shall be stated in SAR. Amounts are stated in Riyals and halalas.

**39. Errors in the Bill**
An arithmetical error in Schedule 1 shall be corrected and shall not vitiate the rate.

## CW p8 (footer: "Page 8")

(Page heading: PART III — PAYMENT, RETENTION AND RECORDS)

**40. Interim valuations**
The Subcontractor shall submit a payment application monthly. Each application shall state the period of the Works it covers, being the first and last day on which any measured item included in it was executed.

**41. Period of an application**
An application covers a period which has closed. No application shall be submitted before the last day of the period it states, and no item shall be included in an application whose stated period does not include the day on which the work was executed. An application shall be submitted within 21 days of the end of the period it covers.

**42. Content of an application**
Each application shall list every item measured, stating the item code, the date of execution, the Site zone, the ground classification where applicable, the quantity, the unit, the built-up rate applied and the resulting amount.

**43. Arithmetic**
The total of an application shall equal the sum of the amounts of the items listed in it. An application whose total does not so agree shall be returned unpaid.

**44. Duplicate measurement**
No item shall be measured twice. Where the same item, work area and date appears more than once in an application, or in more than one application, the later measurement shall be disallowed in full.

**45. Retention**
The Contractor shall retain 5 per cent of the value of each valuation. Retention is calculated on the total of the application and not on individual items, and is rounded down to the nearest halala.

**46. Records as a condition of payment**
Where Schedule 5 states that a record is required for an item, the item is not payable in any valuation until that record has been delivered to the Engineer. The absence of a required record is a breach of this Clause and not merely a want of corroboration.

**47. Form of records**
A record required by Schedule 5 shall identify the work area, the date, the item code and the quantity, and shall be signed by the Subcontractor's foreman and countersigned by the Engineer's representative.

**48. Payment period**
The Contractor shall pay a correctly submitted application within thirty days of its receipt.

**49. Set-off**
The Contractor may set off against any sum due amounts properly due to it under this Subcontract.

**50. Final account**
The final account shall be submitted within ninety days of the Taking-Over Certificate.

**S3. Excavation tolerances**
Formation levels shall be within plus or minus 20 mm of the levels shown. Over-excavation shall be made good with imported granular fill at the Subcontractor's cost and shall not be measured under item A.14.010.

**S4. Classification of excavated material**
Material shall be classified at the point of excavation and recorded on the daily excavation record. A classification not recorded on the day of excavation shall be taken to be G2 Firm Sabkha.

**S13. Pressure testing**
Pressure pipework shall be tested in accordance with the Specification and the certificate delivered before the pipework is included in a valuation.

**S20. Adverse weather**
Work shall be suspended when rainfall exceeds 5 mm in any hour. A suspension shall be recorded on the meteorological record, and standby under item E.51.010 is measurable only for the hours so recorded.

## CW p12 (footer: "Page 12")

(Page heading: PART V — PARTICULAR CONDITIONS)

**P1. Precedence of this Part**
This Part amends the General Conditions. Where a provision of this Part conflicts with Parts I to III, this Part prevails.

**P2. Project-specific working restrictions**
No work shall be executed in Zone Z3 Wadi Crossing during a declared flood watch. Standby during a flood watch is measurable under item E.51.010 and not under item E.51.030.

**P4. Restrictions on night working**
Night work in Zone Z1 Compound requires the written consent of the Contractor by reason of the proximity of occupied accommodation. Consent does not of itself entitle the Subcontractor to the night uplift, which is payable only on the items marked in Schedule 4.

**P6. Statutory undertakers**
Attendance on a statutory undertaker is measured under item E.53.010 and is limited to two days per work area per day of attendance irrespective of the number of undertakers attending.

**P8. Dust suppression**
Dust suppression is deemed included in the rates and is not separately measurable, save that water bowser standing time during a declared dust event is measurable under item E.51.030.

**P9. Working in high temperatures**
Work shall be suspended between 12:00 and 15:00 during the period from 15 June to 15 September in accordance with the applicable labour regulations. Such a suspension is not a ground for standby.

**P10. Ramadan working**
Working hours during Ramadan shall be as agreed in writing before the commencement of the month. An agreed variation of hours does not of itself make work Night Work.
