47A. Site records and their references
A site record is written by the person who carried out the work and is not required to adopt the wording of
the Bill of Quantities. Each record bears a reference from the series stated in Schedule 5 against the item it
evidences, and that reference is quoted against the line in the Application. A record that evidences a week
records each day worked; a week is measurable only where the record shows work on not fewer than five :
days of that week.
