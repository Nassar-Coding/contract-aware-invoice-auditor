CW-2025-0417-CIV | Civil and Earthworks Package :
PART I — GENERAL CONDITIONS ;

1. Definitions
Words and expressions defined in Appendix A shall bear those meanings throughout. Where a word is
used in both the singular and the plural the meaning is unaffected.

2. Subcontract documents :

This Agreement, the General Conditions, Schedules 1 to 5 and Appendix A together constitute the whole
agreement between the parties. In the event of conflict, a Schedule prevails over the General Conditions,
and a later-numbered Schedule prevails over an earlier one. .

3. Scope of the Works

The Subcontractor shall execute the civil and earthworks described in Schedule | at the locations shown on

. the Contract Drawings, together with all work reasonably to be inferred as necessary for their completion.
: 4. The Site

The Site is divided into four zones for the purposes of measurement, described in Schedule 2. The zone
applicable to an item of work is the zone in which the work is physically executed, irrespective of where
materials are stored or plant is based.

5 Ground conditions

° The ground is classified into five categories described in Schedule 3. Classification shall be determined by

the Engineer from the trial pit records and confirmed in writing before the work is executed. A
classification once confirmed shall not be revised retrospectively.

6. Programme _

The Subcontractor shall submit a programme within fourteen days of the Commencement Date and shall
update it monthly.

7. Working hours
Normal working hours are 06:00 to 18:00 on working days. Work executed between 18:00 and 06:00 is
Night Work and attracts the uplift stated in Schedule 4-where, and only where, that Schedule marks the
item as eligible.

8. Rest days : : ,
The rest days under this Subcontract are Friday and Saturday. Work executed on a rest day attracts the

; rest-day uplift stated in Schedule 4 where the item is marked as eligible. No item attracts both the night

uplift and the rest-day uplift on the same measurement unless the Engineer has instructed the work in
writing under Clause 9.

9. Instructed out-of-hours working

The Engineer may instruct work outside normal working hours. An instruction shall be in writing and shall
state whether the night uplift, the rest-day uplift, or both are to apply.

10. Care of the Works

The Subcontractor shall take full responsibility for the care of the Works until the date of the Taking-Over
Certificate.
