P22. Records in electronic form ;
A record required by Schedule 5 may be delivered in electronic form provided it bears both signatures
required by Clause 47. ;
P23. Consequences of a missing record -
Where an item requiring a record under Schedule 5 has been included in a valuation without that record, the
amount shall be deducted from the next valuation together with any retention released upon it.
P24. Valuation of items not in Schedule 1
Work for which no rate appears in Schedule 1 shall be valued at daywork rates in Schedule 6 unless a rate
is agreed in writing before the work is executed. *
P25. Notices ;
A notice under this Subcontract shall be in writing and delivered to the address stated in Appendix A.
