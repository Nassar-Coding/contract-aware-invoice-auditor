33. Surveyed items : :
Items marked as surveyed in Schedule 1 are measured only against a joint survey sheet signed by both
parties. The quantity measured shall be the quantity on the survey sheet exactly. :
34. Provisional quantities : .
: Quantities in Schedule 1 are estimates and do not limit the quantity that may be measured. :
35. Work not measurable
Temporary works, dewatering below the classified water table, and the removal of the Subcontractor's own
waste are deemed included in the rates and are not separately measurable.
36. Disputed measurement
Where the parties cannot agree a measurement, the Engineer's measurement shall be used for the purposes
of interim valuation without prejudice. ;
37. Re-measurement .
The Contractor may re-measure any item within ninety days of the valuation in which it was first included. .
38. Currency
’ All rates are in SAR and all valuations shall be stated in SAR. Amounts are stated in Riyals and halalas.
: 39. Errors in the Bill .
An arithmetical error in Schedule | shall be corrected and shall not vitiate the rate. .
