PART IV — SPECIFICATION PREAMBLES
S1. Setting out and control .
. Primary control stations are as shown on drawing CW-4-SU-001. The Subcontractor shall verify the
stations before commencing and shall report any discrepancy exceeding. 5 mm in plan or 3 mm in level.
$2. Topsoil strip ;
Topsoil shall be stripped to the full depth of the organic horizon and stockpiled separately in the location
shown. Stripping to a depth greater than that instructed shall not be measured. ,
S3. Excavation tolerances
Formation levels shall be within plus or minus 20 mm of the levels shown. Over-excavation shall be made
good with imported granular fill at the Subcontractor’s cost and shall not be measured under item A.14.010. .
S4. Classification of excavated material
Material shall be classified at the point of excavation and recorded on the daily excavation record. A :
. classification not recorded on the day of excavation.shall be taken to be G2 Firm Sabkha.
S5. Disposal
Material classified as unsuitable shall be removed to a licensed tip. Tip tickets shall accompany the payment
application in which the disposal is measured. :
S6. Compaction
Granular fill shall be placed in layers not exceeding 225 mm loose depth and compacted to 95 per cent of
the maximum dry density. One compaction test shall be carried out per 500 m2 per layer. :
S7. Concrete materials .
Cement shall be sulphate-resisting Portland cement. Aggregate shall be from an approved source and shall
be tested for chloride content monthly. oO
. S8. Concrete placing ,
, Concrete shall not be placed when the shade air temperature exceeds 38 degrees Celsius without the written
. approval of the Engineer. Approval for placing at night does not of itself constitute an instruction under
Clause 9.
S9. Curing .
All concrete shall be cured for not less than seven days by an approved method. Curing is deemed included
‘in the rates.
$10. Formwork ~ : :
: Formwork shall be of a quality producing the finish described. Formwork to soffits shall not be struck
before the concrete has attained seventy-five per cent of its characteristic strength. ;
$11. Reinforcement a :
Reinforcement shall be measured by the theoretical mass of the bar schedule. Laps not shown on the
schedule and tying wire, spacers and chairs are deemed included and are not separately measurable. -
