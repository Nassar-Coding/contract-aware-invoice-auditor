PART V — PARTICULAR CONDITIONS

P1. Precedence of this Part ; ;

This Part amends the General Conditions. Where a provision of this Part conflicts with Parts I to III, this
Part prevails.

P2. Project-specific working restrictions

: No work shall be executed in Zone Z3 Wadi Crossing during a declared flood watch. Standby during a

flood watch is measurable under item E.51.010 and not under item E.51.030.

P3. Access to the Escarpment platform

Zone Z4 Escarpment is served by a single-track haul road. The Subcontractor shall programme deliveries to
avoid simultaneous movements and shall not claim standing time arising from failure to do so.

P4. Restrictions on night working :

Night work in Zone Z1 Compound requires the written consent of the Contractor by reason of the
proximity of occupied accommodation. Consent does not of itself entitle the Subcontractor to the night
uplift, which is payable only on the items marked in Schedule 4.

P5. Existing services :

The Subcontractor shall carry out a service scan before any excavation and shall record the result. Damage
to a service shown on the record is at the Subcontractor's cost.

P6. Statutory undertakers .

Attendance on a statutory undertaker is measured under item E.53.010 and is limited to two days per work
area per day of attendance irrespective of the number of undertakers attending. :

P7. Archaeological finds

Work shall cease immediately on the discovery of a find of archaeological interest and shall not resume
without the written instruction of the Engineer.

: P8. Dust suppression :

Dust suppression is deemed included in the rates and is not separately measurable, save that water bowser
standing time during a declared dust event is measurable under item E.51.030. :

P9. Working in high temperatures : A
Work shall be suspended between 12:00 and 15:00 during the period from 15 June to 15 September in
accordance with the applicable labour regulations. Such a suspension is not a ground for standby.

P10. Ramadan working :
Working hours during Ramadan shall be as agreed in writing before the commencement of the month. An
agreed variation of hours does not of itself make work Night Work.
