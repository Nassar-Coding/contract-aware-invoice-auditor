PART III — PAYMENT, RETENTION AND RECORDS :
40. Interim valuations ;
The Subcontractor shall submit a payment application monthly. Each application shall state the period of
the Works it covers, being the first and last day on which any measured item included in it was executed.
41. Period of an application : : .
An application covers a period which has closed. No application shall be submitted before the last day of
the period it states, and no item shall be included in an application whose stated period does not include the
. day on which the work was executed. An application shall be submitted within 21 days of the end of the
period it covers.
42. Content of an application
Each application shall list every item measured, stating the item code, the date of execution, the Site zone,
the ground classification where applicable, the quantity, the unit, the built-up rate applied and the resulting
amount.
43. Arithmetic .
The total of an application shall equal the sum of the amounts of the items listed in it. An application whose
: total does not so agree shall be returned unpaid. :
44. Duplicate measurement :
No item shall be measured twice. Where the same item, work area and date appears more than once in an
application, or in more than one application, the later measurement shall be disallowed in full.
45. Retention
The Contractor shall retain 5 per cent of the value of each valuation. Retention is calculated on the total of
the application and not on individual items, and is rounded down to the nearest halala.
: 46. Records as a condition of payment
Where Schedule 5 states that a record is required for an item, the item is not payable in any valuation until
that record has been delivered to the Engineer. The absence of a required record is a breach of this Clause
and not merely a want of corroboration.
. 47. Form of records
A record required by Schedule 5 shall identify the work area, the date, the item code and the quantity, and
shall be signed by the Subcontractor's foreman and countersigned by the Engineer's representative.
48. Payment period ;
The Contractor shall pay a correctly submitted application within thirty days of its receipt.
49. Set-off ;
The Contractor may set off against any sum due amounts properly due to it under this Subcontract.
50. Final account
The final account shall be submitted within ninety days of the Taking-Over Certificate. : .
