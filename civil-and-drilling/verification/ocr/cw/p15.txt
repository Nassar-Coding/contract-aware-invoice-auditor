PART VI — HEALTH, SAFETY AND ENVIRONMENT .
H1. Health and safety management P
The Subcontractor shall appoint a full-time safety officer for the duration of the Works and shall maintain a
. safety file available for inspection at all times. .
H2. Method statements and risk assessments ,
No activity shall commence before an approved method statement and risk assessment have been accepted
: by the Engineer. Work executed without an accepted method statement shall not be measured.
H3. Permits to work ;
A permit to work is required for excavation exceeding 1.2 m in depth, for any work in a confined space, for
hot work, and for work within 3 m of a live service. :
H4. Excavation support - : : ,
No person shall enter an excavation exceeding 1.2 m in depth unless the sides are supported or battered to a
safe angle. Earthwork support is measured under items A.15.010 and A.15.020.
. -H5. Personal protective equipment ; : :
Safety helmet, safety footwear, high-visibility clothing and eye protection shall be worn at all times on the
Site. :
H6. Heat stress . ; ;
The Subcontractor shall implement the heat stress management plan, including the midday break required .
: by Clause P9 and the provision of shaded rest areas and drinking water. :
H7. Lifting operations ;
All lifting operations shall be planned by an appointed person and supervised by a competent lift
supervisor.
H8. Traffic and pedestrian segregation
Vehicle routes and pedestrian routes shall be physically segregated. A banksman shall be provided for :
every reversing movement. : _- "8
H9. Accident reporting
Any accident shall be reported to the Contractor within one hour and in writing within twenty-four hours,
H10. Environmental management
The Subcontractor shall comply with the Project Environmental Management Plan and shall not discharge
any substance to ground or watercourse. .
H11. Waste
Waste shall be segregated and removed to a licensed facility. Waste transfer notes shall be retained for the ;
duration of the Works and produced on request. ~
