S18. Kerbs ;
Kerbs shall be bedded on a concrete foundation and haunched to the detail shown. The foundation and
haunching are deemed included. * -
$19. Road marking ae |
Thermoplastic material shall be applied to a clean dry surface. Marking applied to a surface course less than
fourteen days old shall be re-applied at the Subcontractor's cost.

$20. Adverse weather ;
Work shall be suspended when rainfall exceeds 5 mm in any hour. A suspension shall be recorded on the
meteorological record, and standby under item E.51.010 is measurable only for the hours so recorded. :
