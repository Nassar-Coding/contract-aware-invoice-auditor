11. Setting out

The Subcontractor shall set out the Works from the primary control stations. Setting out is measured under __-
item E.52.010 by chainage band and only against a joint survey signed by both parties.

12. Materials and workmanship

All materials shall be new and of the qualities described. Materials not described shall be of a quality
approved by the Engineer. :

13. Testing

The Subcontractor shall carry out the tests described in the Specification. Where Schedule 5 makes a test
certificate a condition of payment, no valuation shall include the item until the certificate has been
delivered. -

14. Defective work :

The Engineer may instruct the removal and re-execution of defective work. Re-execution is at the :
Subcontractor's cost and shall not be measured again.

15. Variations

The Engineer may instruct variations. A varied item shall be valued at the rates in Schedule 1 where the
character and conditions of the work are not substantially changed. :

16. Provisional sums

Provisional sums shall be expended only on the written instruction of the Engineer and shall be omitted
from the Subcontract Sum if not so instructed.

17. Dayworks

Work instructed to be executed on a daywork basis shall be recorded on daywork sheets signed daily by the
Engineer's representative. Daywork sheets not signed on the day of the work shall not be admitted.

18. Plant and standing time

Standing time is not payable except (a) where the Works are suspended by reason of adverse weather, in
which case item E.51.010 applies against the meteorological record, and (b) plant standing measured under
item E.51.030 against the plant standing record, each as required by Schedule 5. ,

_ 19. Traffic management

Traffic management is measured by the day under item E.51.020. Not more than one day of traffic
management is measurable per work area per day, irrespective of the number of locations at which signals
are deployed.

20. Health and safety

The Subcontractor shall comply with the Project Health and Safety Plan.

: 21. Insurance :
The Subcontractor shall maintain the insurances described in Appendix A throughout the period of the
Works. . ;
