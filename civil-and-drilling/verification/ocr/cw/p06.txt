PART II — MEASUREMENT AND VALUATION
25. Principle of measurement . ,
The Works shall be measured net as executed in accordance with the Civil Engineering Standard Method of
Measurement, as modified by these Clauses. Where a Clause of this Part conflicts with the Method of .
Measurement, this Part prevails.
26. Units of measurement :
Each item in Schedule | states the unit in which it is measured. No item shall be measured in any other
: unit, and a quantity presented in a unit other than that stated shall be rejected in its entirety rather than ;
converted. . :
27. Build-up of rates . . .
The rates in Schedule 1 are base rates. The sum payable for a measured quantity is ascertained by applying
to the base rate, in the following order and no other: first the site zone factor from Schedule 2; then the
ground classification factor from Schedule 3; then the night uplift, if applicable; then the rest-day uplift, if
applicable; then the cumulative quantity rebate from Schedule 4, if the threshold has been passed; and the
resulting sum is then rounded.
28. Rounding .
Rounding shall be applied once only, to the rate after the whole of the build-up described in Clause 27 has
been completed, to the nearest halala, a half halala being rounded upward. Rates shall not be rounded at
intermediate steps. The amount for a measured quantity is the quantity multiplied by the rounded rate; .
where Clause 30 divides a quantity at a rebate threshold, each part is multiplied by its own rounded rate and
the amount is their sum. :
29. Application of factors
: A factor applies to an item only where Schedule 2 or Schedule 3 lists that item's series. An item whose
series is not listed is measured at the base rate irrespective of the zone or ground classification in which it is
: executed.
30. Cumulative quantity rebates
Where Schedule 4 states a rebate threshold for an item, the rebate applies to that part of the quantity which
exceeds the threshold when taken cumulatively across the whole of the Works to date, and not to the
quantity below it. The cumulative count runs from the Commencement Date, is not reset by a valuation,
and is taken in order of the date of execution; measurements executed on the same date are taken in order of
application number and then line number. .
31. Daily limitations :
Where Schedule 4 states a daily limitation for an item, no more than that quantity is measurable for that
item, per work area, per day. A quantity in excess of the limitation is not payable and shall not be carried
forward to a subsequent day.
: ; 32. Mutually exclusive items , Ee
7 Where Schedule 4 states that an item is excluded by another, the excluded item is not measurable within the :
stated number of days following the measurement of the excluding item on the same work area.
