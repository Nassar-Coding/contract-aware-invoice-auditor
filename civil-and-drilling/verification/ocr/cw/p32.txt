PART VII — PARTICULAR CONDITIONS (SECOND SERIES) —
3 3A. Contract Years : :
The first Contract Year runs from the Commencement Date to the day before its first anniversary. Each
following Contract Year runs from that anniversary. The final Contract Year ends on the Date for
Completion as extended by any Amendment, however long that leaves it; an extension of time does not
begin a new Contract Year. . . :
6A. Chargeable Hour i .
Where an item is measured by the hour, a Chargeable Hour is an hour of attendance other than the first
hour of that attendance. The site record states the hours attended; the quantity measured is one hour fewer.
26A. Rates stated in another currency
Where Schedule | states a rate in USD, the rate is converted at the rate of exchange published in Schedule
2B for the calendar month in which the work was executed, and the converted figure is rounded to the
nearest halala with a half rounded to the nearer even halala. The conversion is made before any factor or ‘
uplift is applied. :
27A. Conditions on the uplifts and factors
The night-working uplift is not payable on an item priced at a zone factor exceeding 1.1, the allowance for
working away from the compound being taken to include it. In any period of extension of time granted by
an Amendment, that is to say for work executed after 27 September 2025, the ground classification is taken
as G2 Firm Sabkha whatever the Engineer recorded on the day.
29A. Indexed rates d
The rate for an item listed in Schedule 2A is the base rate stated in Schedule 1 multiplied by the Site
Materials Index published for the calendar month in which the work was executed and divided by the base
; index, and rounded to the nearest halala with a half rounded to the nearer even halala. That figure is then :
treated as the rate for the purposes of Clause 27, and Clause 28 applies to the build-up as usual.
31A. Instruments taking effect before their date of issue ;
Where an Amendment substitutes a rate with effect from a date before the date on which it was issued, an
Application for Payment submitted before that date of issue is not thereby incorrect: it was valued at the
rate then in force. The difference between the rates on work already valued shall be shown as a single
adjustment on the first Application for Payment submitted on or after the date of issue, and on no other.
33A. Tolerance on measurement
Where an item is measured against a joint survey, a measured quantity not exceeding the surveyed quantity
by more than 2 per cent is payable as measured. A measured quantity exceeding the surveyed quantity by
more than that is payable at the surveyed quantity only.
45A. Release of retention
On the first Application for Payment submitted after the Date for Completion as extended, one half of the
retention held on all earlier Applications is released, rounded down to the halala. The amount payable on an
: Application is the measured total, plus any adjustment under Clause 31A, less the retention deducted on it,
‘plus any retention released under this Clause. ;
