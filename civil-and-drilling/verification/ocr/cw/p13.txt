a P13. Measurement of trench depth .

of the pipe, and shall determine which of items A.12.020, A.12.030 and A.12.040 applies. Where a single

; trench spans two depth bands each part shall be measured separately.

Where the frequency of testing stated in the Specification is exceeded at the Engineer's instruction, the

P18. Reinforcement wastage , :
Wastage on reinforcement is deemed included. The quantity measured is the theoretical mass on the ;
approved bar schedule (Clause 33). ' :
P19. Drainage testing before backfill
No length of drainage shall be backfilled before the pressure test has been witnessed. Item A.14.020 is not
measurable within two days of the measurement of item A.14.010 on the same work area (Schedule 4, Part
5).
P20. Surfacing in adverse weather
Surface course shall not be laid when the road surface temperature is below 10 degrees Celsius or during
rainfall.

Traffic management for surfacing is deemed included in the surfacing rates. Item E.51.020 is not

. measurable for a work area on a day on which any of items D.41.020, D.41.030, D.41.050, D.43.010 or
D.43.020 is measured for that work area. :
