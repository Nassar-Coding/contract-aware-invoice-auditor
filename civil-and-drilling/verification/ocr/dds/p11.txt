PART VI — PARTICULAR CONDITIONS
P1. Precedence :
This Part amends Parts I to V and prevails over them. : :
P2. High pressure, high temperature wells ;
A well is HPHT where the call-off so states; the class-rated factor applies to the whole well and not only to
the deeper sections. :
P3. Extended reach wells
A well is Extended Reach where the call-off so states, whatever the horizontal displacement achieved. :
__ P4. Rig moves .
No service is charged for a day on which the rig is moving between wells; such days are not days on the
well. . : .
PS. Weather .
A day lost to weather is recorded as Standby on the Daily Drilling Report.
P6. Tool failure :
: Where a tool fails in the hole, the day remains an Operating day for the other services; the failed tool is
: charged only on days the report records it in the hole.
’ P7. Crew changes . ;
A person on a crew change day is counted once, whether arriving or departing. ;
P8. Night working ~ : :
No uplift is payable for work at night; the rates in Schedule 1 cover continuous operations. ~
P9. Public holidays ,
No uplift is payable for work on a public holiday. , a .
P10. Minimum hours : ; :
The minimum in Clause 21 applies to each Operating day separately and is not averaged over a run.
P11. Discount basis
The discount under Clause 38 is calculated on each invoice alone and not on the Contractor's invoices taken
together. : :
; P12. Lost in hole hours : :
Circulating hours for Clause 31 are those the tool accumulated on the well on which it was lost, including
the day of the loss. :
P13. Standby personnel . ;
Personnel are charged on a Standby day at the standby percentage for their position in Schedule 3 Part 3.
