PART IV — INVOICING AND PAYMENT
32. Invoices ‘
: The Contractor shall invoice each well separately, for a period of consecutive days on the well. Every
charge on an invoice is for a day within the period the invoice states. :
33. Submission :
An invoice shall not be submitted before the last day of its period and shall be submitted within 30 days
after it. , i
34. Content of a charge : :
Each charge states the date, the service code, the description, the hole section, the status of the day, the
quantity, the unit, the rate and the amount, and for item PD-210 the depths at which the charged interval
starts and ends. :
35. Units ; ;
A charge is stated in the unit Schedule 1 gives for the service, and in no other.
36. Net amount : : :
The net amount of an invoice is the sum of the amounts of its charges, including the discount charge under .
Clause 38.
* 37. Documents as a condition of payment , :
A charge for a service Schedule 5 lists is:not payable until the document Schedule 5 names has been
delivered for the day or run the charge is for.
38. Discount . : ’
Where the sum of the amounts of the services charged on an invoice exceeds 250,000.00 USD, the :
Contractor allows a discount of-4 per cent of the excess, rounded under Clause 17, stated as a single charge
under code DS-900 with a negative amount. : :
39. Value Added Tax .
Value Added Tax is charged at 15 per cent of the net amount of the invoice, rounded under Clause 17.
40. Invoice total
The total of an invoice is its net amount plus Value Added Tax. ;
41. Disputed charges
The Company may withhold a disputed charge and shall pay the undisputed balance.
42. Payment .
The Company shall pay within forty-five days of receipt of a correct invoice. 1
