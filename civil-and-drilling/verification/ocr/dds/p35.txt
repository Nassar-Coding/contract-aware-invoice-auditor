PART IX — PARTICULAR CONDITIONS (SECOND SERIES)
3A. Contract Years ,
The first Contract Year runs from the Commencement Date to the day before its first anniversary, and each
following Contract Year from that anniversary. The final Contract Year ends on the Expiry Date as
extended by any Amendment; an extension does not begin a new Contract Year. :
17A. Indexed rates
The rate for a service listed in Schedule 2C is the base rate stated in Schedule 1 multiplied by the Rig
Services Index published for the calendar month in which the service was performed and divided by the
base index, rounded half to even. That figure is the rate for the purposes of Clause 17.
17B. Factors that do not apply
The hole-section factor in Schedule 3 Part | is not applied on a day the rig is on Standby, the section not
being drilled. The well-class factor in Schedule 3 Part 2 is not applied to item PD-210, Schedule 2 having
already priced the metre by depth.
19A. The Daily Drilling Report and its number
Each Daily Drilling Report bears a number in the series DDR, and that number is quoted against every line
of that day on the invoice. The report is written by the Contractor's supervisor in the ordinary words of the
rig and does not repeat the descriptions in Schedule 1 or carry the service codes.
21A. Chargeable Hour
For a service charged by the hour, the first hour of each period in the hole is rig-up and is not a Chargeable
Hour. The report states the hours run; the invoice charges one fewer.
25A. Tolerance on metres
Metres charged not exceeding the metres the report supports by more than 1 per cent are payable as
charged. Metres exceeding that are payable at the figure the report supports.
31A. Replacement values
The replacement values in Schedule 2D are stated in SAR and are converted into the currency of the
Contract at the rate of exchange published in that Schedule for the month in which the tool was lost,
rounded half to even, before the depreciation in Clause 31 is applied. Schedule 6 states the same values in
the currency of the Contract as they stood when it was let, and Schedule 2D governs.
36A. Amendments taking effect before their date of issue
Where an Amendment substitutes a rate with effect from a date before the date it was issued, an invoice
submitted before that date of issue was correct at the rate then in force. The difference on services already
invoiced is shown as a single adjustment on the first invoice submitted on or after the date of issue, and on
no other.
