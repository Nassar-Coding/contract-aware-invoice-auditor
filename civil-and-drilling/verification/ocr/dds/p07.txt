26. Charges per run :
Item DD-111 is charged once for each BHA run carrying a positive displacement motor, on the last day of
that run. Item LW-420 is charged once for each BHA run carrying a radioactive source, on the first day of
that run.

27. Charges per well .

Items MB-701 and DD-140 are charged once for the well, on the first day on the well. Items LW-430 and

MB-702 are charged once for the well, on the last day on the well; item LW-430 only where logging while

drilling was run. .

28. Tools in the hole .

A rental charged by the day is charged for a day only where the Daily Drilling Report records the tool in

the hole on that day.

29. No double charging

No service is charged twice for the same well and the same day, save that item PD-210 may be charged

more than once on a day for different depth intervals under Clause 23.

30. Counts from the report

Gyro surveys, formation pressure points, wiper trips, back-reaming hours and clean-out runs are charged in
: the numbers recorded on the Daily Drilling Report for an Operating day.

31. Lost in hole

Where a tool cannot be recovered, the charge is its replacement value in Schedule 6 less depreciation of 1

per cent for each complete 25 circulating hours the tool accumulated on the well, as stated on the Lost in

Hole Report, to a depreciation of not more than 50 per cent. The result is rounded under Clause 17 and

charged as one unit on the day of the loss.
