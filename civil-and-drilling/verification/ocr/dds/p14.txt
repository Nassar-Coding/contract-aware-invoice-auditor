PART VIII — REPORTING AND DATA
R1. Reporting responsibility :
The lead directional driller is responsible for the accuracy of the Contractor's entries on the Daily Drilling
Report. )
R2. Time of issue . ; :
The Daily Drilling Report for a day is issued by 06:00 on the following day. .
R3. Depths : :
Measured depths are stated in whole metres from the rotary table. _ :
R4. Circulating hours _
- Circulating hours are stated in whole hours, any part hour of thirty minutes or more counting as a whole .
hour.
RS. Tools in the hole :
The report lists by service code every rental tool in the hole at any time during the day.
R6. Personnel
The report states, by service code, the number of the Contractor's personnel on the rig during the day.
R7. Status
The status of the day is recorded by the Company Representative and is final for the purposes of Clause 19.
-  _R8. Corrections :
A report may be corrected only by a revision signed by both signatories before the invoice for the day is __
submitted. .
R9. BHA Run Report ,
The BHA Run Report is issued within two days of the end of the run and totals the circulating hours and
metres of the Daily Drilling Reports for the run. ‘ .
R10. Survey data : ;
Survey data are delivered in the Company's format within twelve hours of each survey.
R11. Log data ; ; , ; .
LWD data are delivered in real time where the tools allow and in memory form at the end of each run. ~
R12. Retention of records .
The Contractor shall retain all field records for seven years after the end of the Contract.
